CampusFix Problem Statement
 1. Introduction

CampusFix is a proposed software system for reporting and managing maintenance problems within a university.

In a university environment, students and staff may encounter problems such as faulty computers, damaged projectors, broken lights, water leaks, or faulty classroom doors. These problems may be reported informally through verbal communication, phone calls, WhatsApp messages, emails, or paper forms.

 2. Problem

The current informal approach makes it difficult for the university to properly track maintenance requests. It may be difficult to know which problems have been reported, which ones are still unresolved, who is responsible for handling them, and how long each request has been waiting.

It can also be difficult to identify urgent problems and monitor whether maintenance requests are being handled within the expected response time.
 3. Intended Users

CampusFix is intended to serve:

- Students who need to report maintenance problems.
- University staff who report faults in their workplaces.
- Maintenance staff who receive and resolve maintenance requests.
- University administrators who need to monitor maintenance activities.

 4. Proposed Solution

CampusFix will provide a centralized system where students and staff can report maintenance problems. The system will record each request and allow it to be prioritized, assigned to maintenance personnel, updated as work progresses, and eventually closed when the problem has been resolved.

This will provide a more organized way of managing maintenance requests and make it easier to track the progress of each reported problem.

## 5. Goal of CampusFix

The main goal of CampusFix is to improve the way university maintenance problems are reported, tracked, assigned, and resolved.

The system should provide better visibility of outstanding requests, their priorities, assigned maintenance staff, and their current status.
